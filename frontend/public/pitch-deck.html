How it works

Victim upload to court PDF in four steps

STEP 01

Pre-parse SHA-256

The file is hashed with Web Crypto before a single byte is parsed.

STEP 02

IST graph mapping

Rows normalised to UTR, amount and IST time, then mapped onto the case graph.

STEP 03

Dilution & risk

New ratio = dirty received ÷ total balance post-receipt. +20 mixer, +15 fan-out.

STEP 04

Sec 63(4) court PDF

Part A (IO) + Part B (examiner), hash table and dilution annexure.
